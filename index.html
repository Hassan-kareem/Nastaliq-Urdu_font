Critical Safety Rule (Urdu Lang Setup)

Do NOT drag Urdu to the #1 position in your System Language settings!

On Android 15, 16, and 17, making a heavy font like Jameel Noori your first (primary) system language will crash SystemUI / Skia engine, leading to a black screen loop.

Correct Way: Keep English (or your default language) at position #1, and add Urdu at position #2. Urdu text in all apps will still automatically show in Nastaliq!

How to Install & Configure

01

Flash the Module

Download the zip for your favorite variant and flash it using Magisk Manager or KernelSU.

02

Configure Settings

Navigate to Settings > System > Languages. Add Urdu, but keep it dragged below English (Language #2).

03

Reboot & Enjoy

Reboot your phone. Open any app like WhatsApp or Chrome, and all Urdu text will be rendered in premium Nastaliq layout!

Crash Recovery

If you mistakenly set Urdu as Language #1 and get stuck in a bootloop or black screen, boot into custom recovery (TWRP/OrangeFox) and delete the module directory:

For Standard Jameel Noori variants:

rm -rf /data/adb/modules/Nastaliq_Urdu

For Noto Alt (Naskh swap) variant:

rm -rf /data/adb/modules/NotoNastaliqUrdu-alt

For Mehr Alt (Naskh swap) variant:

rm -rf /data/adb/modules/Mehr_Nastaliq-alt

After executing the command, reboot your phone normally and the system will start up correctly with the module removed.
